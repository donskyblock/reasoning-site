# Why me and many others are leaving discord

As of recently Discord has decided to announce a change of enforcing global id verifcation, heavily restricting those who refuse to ID Verify. I personally enjoy discord as a platform so this saddens me to my core that this is occuring for obvious reasons. But overall the change is a negative one. Alongside many other small things such as discord's plans to implement AI and go public we have taken this as a sign of a coming to of a end for an era for discord. Overall I would love to continue discord but the changes coming are something I can not ignore. This statement has been agreed upon by the many I have talked to over the past week or so. So I have decided alongside many others to step away from the platform as a whole and seek alternatives.

## What we’re using instead

I have developed a platform name OpenCom, which is designed to allow those who use discord to switch easily whilst also being to some degree decenteralised and open source. The codebase for this platform is fully open source but we do have a subscription service to assist with the funding of running the core services. As servers themselves can be hosted by the community but we do offer a singular server for free per account or a unlimited amount if you use the subscription.

Anyways beyond my pitch Thats the jist of it.
